# Keyboards & Mice

The whole input stack, as it sits on the desk: two Vortex 40% keyboards, a QMK-modded ZSA Moonlander, an Apple trackpad, and a single-button Ploopy trackball. There is no mouse — and the layout, not any one device, is the interesting part.

## The keyboards

- **Two Vortex 40%s.** I have two of them; the desk uses one. Small, dense, and exactly the size the layout below needs.
- **A QMK-modded ZSA Moonlander.** The big one — split into two halves, running QMK the way I want it to. The layout is built around it.

## The layout

The desk is a sandwich: the **Vortex sits between the two halves of the Moonlander**, the 40% in the middle and the split flanking it.

Two more pieces fall out of that arrangement:

- **An A5 cutting mat under the Vortex.** The desk doubles as the printer's post-processing station — supports get trimmed and prints get cleaned up on the mat, right under the keyboard. The [A8](/printer) lives on the workbench; this is its cleanup counter.
- **The Ploopy under the Moonlander's bottom-right, by the thumb cluster.** One button, a trackball, and it lives where the right hand already is — within reach without leaving the keyboard, which is the whole point.

## The pointer

Daily pointer work goes through the **Apple trackpad** — clicking, scrolling, dragging, the usual. The **Ploopy Nano 2** is the drawing tool: a minimalist, single-button, 3D-printable trackball. The work behind it — the [QMK fork](/projects#qmk-ploopy) and the [nano-2 trackball](/projects#nano-2-trackball) — is on the projects page.

No mouse anywhere in the picture. Trackpad for the computer, trackball for the drawing.
